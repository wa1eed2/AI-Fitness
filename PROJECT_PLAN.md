# AI-Fitness Project Plan

## Goal

Build an evidence-based fitness and nutrition platform that uses
research, user data, biometrics, and AI to provide personalized
fitness and nutrition guidance.

## User Assessment

The application will collect information such as:

- Age
- Height
- Weight
- Activity level
- Fitness level
- Training experience
- Training frequency
- Fitness goals
- Available equipment
- Exercise preferences
- Exercises the user dislikes
- Sleep and recovery information
- Optional biometric information
- Current strength/performance data
- Pain or physical limitations
- Dietary preferences
- Food allergies
- Foods the user avoids
- Foods the user enjoys
- Budget and cooking preferences

## Personalized Recommendations

The application should use the user's information to recommend:

- Appropriate fitness goals
- Exercise selection
- Workout routines
- Training frequency
- Exercise alternatives
- Progression strategies
- Nutrition recommendations
- Meal ideas
- Recovery strategies
- Fitness tracking metrics

Recommendations should consider the user's goals, fitness level,
available equipment, preferences, limitations, and relevant research.

Pain and physical limitations should be treated as safety constraints.
The application should not diagnose injuries or medical conditions.

## Research-Based Fitness Questions

The application will use a curated research database to answer
questions about:

- Hypertrophy
- Strength
- Resistance training
- Exercise selection
- Training volume
- Training frequency
- Recovery
- Sleep
- Nutrition
- Supplements
- Weight management
- Sports nutrition

Answers should provide relevant sources and communicate uncertainty
when the available evidence is limited or conflicting.

## Workout System

- Personalized workout generation
- Exercise recommendations
- Exercise substitutions
- User exercise preferences
- Workout logging
- Sets, reps, and weight tracking
- Progressive overload tracking
- Training volume analysis
- Strength progression

## Nutrition System

- Nutrition recommendations
- Meal planning
- Food preferences
- Allergies
- Foods to avoid
- Favorite foods
- Dietary patterns
- Budget considerations
- Nutrition tracking

## Progress Tracking

- Bodyweight tracking
- Strength progression
- Training volume
- Workout consistency
- Optional body measurements
- Progress photos
- Progress comparisons over time

## Computer Vision

Future versions may include:

- Progress photo organization and comparison
- Exercise form analysis
- Movement analysis

Image analysis should focus on observable information and progress,
not judging a person's appearance.
