## Questions Addressed

For each research paper, record:

- What question does the research investigate?
- What population was studied?
- What intervention or exposure was studied?
- What was the comparison?
- What outcomes were measured?
- What were the main findings?
- What are the potential benefits?
- Under what conditions were the benefits observed?
- What are the limitations?
- What conclusions did the researchers reach?
- How applicable are the findings to the target user?

## Research-to-Recommendation

The database should eventually connect research findings to
fitness and nutrition questions.

Research Paper
↓
Research Topic
↓
Research Question
↓
Study Findings
↓
Evidence Assessment
↓
Potential Practical Application
↓
AI-Fitness Recommendation

Recommendations should not be created solely from a single research
paper. When possible, the system should consider multiple relevant
sources and the quality and limitations of the evidence.
